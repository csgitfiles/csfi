Fixture Forecast

Fixtures are assembled to order at the NJ warehouse from modules, optics, housings, and nodes already tracked elsewhere in this tool — there's no separate fixture-level procurement or MOQ. This tab exists to forecast demand by finished-product type, which drives housing consumption on the next tab. Any module type fits any fixture, so fixture mix is independent of the module mix.

Housing Ordering Plan

Brass housings, ordered from the China factory in large infrequent batches (1–4×/year) — not the Lutron monthly cadence used for modules/optics/nodes. 120-day lead time · safety stock set at a 30-day supply · 200-unit MOQ. Reorder triggers when projected inventory minus the next 4 months of consumption would fall below the safety floor.
