Fixture Forecast

Fixtures are assembled to order at the NJ warehouse from modules, optics, housings, and nodes already tracked elsewhere in this tool, so there's no separate fixture-level procurement or MOQ. This tab exists to forecast demand by finished-product type, which drives housing consumption on the next tab. Any module type fits any fixture, so fixture mix is independent of the module mix.
