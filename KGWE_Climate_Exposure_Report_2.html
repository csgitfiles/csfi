Methodology

Climate figures come from WeatherSpark's long-run reconstructions (hourly weather, 1980–2016, NASA MERRA-2 reanalysis blended with the nearest airport/city stations) for the city matching each ZIP code. "Typical" is the range daily highs/lows normally sit in across the year. "Rare" is the 10th/90th‑percentile extreme — roughly the coldest and hottest conditions reached on about one day in ten. Where a ZIP had no dedicated station, the nearest comparable city or airport was used and is noted in the table. One order line (Bravas Boca Raton) had no ZIP on file and was estimated from the dealer's Boca Raton market.

Reading the "exposure" flag

Exposure measures how many °C the site's rare cold/hot extreme sits outside the KGWE's rated 0–40°C band. This is outdoor ambient climate, used as a proxy for unconditioned equipment spaces (garages, attics, outdoor closets) — not a measurement of the actual enclosure temperature. Treat it as a screening signal for which dealers are worth a direct check-in, not as confirmed field failure data.
