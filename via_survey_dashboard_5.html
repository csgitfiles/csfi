What reps say they need

Support & resource requests, by firm
Bell & McCoy Integrated Solutions
Training for their team and dealers.
Market Share
Samples/demo units for events and reps' vans, plus in-market CS staff support at dealer events.
Hi-Tech Sales & Marketing
Pricing, webinars, literature, and support tools.
NuTech Group
Help support the NuTech Experience Center as a VIA showcase.
Zone Pacific Sales
Add VIA to the design studio; create a one-page system diagram showing all necessary components. Suggests a basic demo kit built for reps' vans.
Atlantic Integrated
Samples, an introductory promotion, training modules, a Coastal Current session, and press & marketing support.
AWA Reps
Additional training. Proposes a VIA-only showcase event in Chicago to kick things off.
LK
Add VIA to Design Studio Express, plus demo kits made available for reps.
High-Tech Network
Demo product for the experience center, an aggressive demo deal for existing dealers, and marketing videos with case studies.
Unified Representatives
Strategic in-market experience events (citing past success with "Dine Under Ketra"), plus an aggressive spec-community program with low-cost product access to build pipeline.
Accessreps
A one-pager showing how VIA integrates into a full system.
Progressive Rockies
Peer review, case studies, job examples, and training on selling color-change capability.
Performance Sales & Marketing
Pricing and literature, ideally a colored brochure — notes it's hard to gauge demand fully until support material and pricing are finalized.
